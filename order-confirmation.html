Featured Kitchen Recipe

The Kabod Crest Heritage Egusi & Ugwu Pot

A celebration of golden melon seeds, aromatic uziza, savory stockfish, and vibrant fluted pumpkin leaves.

Pantry Ingredients (From This Store):

  • 2 cups Kabod Crest Melon Seed (Egusi)
  • 1 pack Kabod Crest Dehydrated Ugwu (warm-water soaked for 5 minutes)
  • 1 tablespoon Kabod Crest Fermented Iru (Locust Beans)
  • 1 tablespoon Kabod Crest Ashanti Pepper (Uziza)
  • 2 pieces Kabod Crest Stockfish Cod & Ponmo
  • Serve alongside Kabod Crest Cassava Flakes (Garri)

Preparation Steps:

  1. Slow-simmer stockfish and ponmo in water with fermented Iru for 15 minutes to build a rich savory broth foundation.
  2. Blend melon seeds (egusi) with warm water into a thick paste; drop in gentle lumps into the simmering broth.
  3. Allow the egusi lumps to set and gently puff up without excessive stirring so they form tender curd pillows.
  4. Stir in stone-ground Ashanti pepper (uziza) and fold in the rehydrated Ugwu leaves during the final 3 minutes of cooking.
