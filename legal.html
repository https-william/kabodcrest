1. Delivery & Logistics Policy

At Kabod Crest Foods, we prioritize parcel freshness and clear communication across all order dispatches.

  • Pay on Delivery for Courier Fees: Online checkout charges strictly cover your packaged pantry goods. Because freight rates vary based on exact destination address, distance, and parcel weight, delivery fees are paid directly to the courier service upon parcel handover.
  • Lagos Orders: Prepared and dispatched from our Lagos packing facility. Standard Lagos dispatch takes 24 to 48 hours following payment confirmation.
  • Nationwide Deliveries: Dispatched via verified interstate logistics partners. Estimated arrival is 2 to 4 business days depending on state capital or regional destination.
  • Diaspora Air Freight: For diaspora customers in the UK, US, and Canada, shipments are consolidated and dispatched via international air freight. International shipping quotes and customs duties guidance are confirmed prior to flight dispatch.

2. Food Safety & Quality Standards

We believe every leaf and spice entering your kitchen must meet rigorous standards of hygiene, botanical purity, and traceability.

  • Multi-Stage Hand Sorting: Fresh harvests of fluted pumpkin leaves (Ugwu), ginger rhizomes, and raw spices are hand-sorted to eliminate debris, stalks, and imperfect specimens.
  • Thorough Multi-Washing: Fresh produce undergoes clean multi-stage water washing under strict internal hygiene protocols before entering dehydration.
  • Gentle Low-Temperature Dehydration: Produce is dehydrated in closed hygienic dryers below 45 degrees Celsius. This preserves delicate natural chlorophyll, aromatic essential oils, and vital heat-sensitive vitamins without chemical additives or preservatives.
  • Moisture-Proof Packaging: Finished products are sealed immediately into heavy-gauge, multi-layer barrier standup pouches with airtight zip seals to protect against humidity and ambient light.
  • Regulatory Compliance: We maintain strict internal food-grade protocols aligned with Good Manufacturing Practice (GMP) requirements as part of our formal NAFDAC product registration procedures.

3. Returns, Replacements & Refunds

Because we sell edible food products, food safety regulations prohibit us from accepting returns of opened pouches. However, we stand behind the condition of every dispatch.

  • 7-Day Replacement Window: If an item arrives with damaged packaging, an unsealed barrier pouch, or an incorrect product, notify us within 7 days of delivery with photos of the sealed packaging and batch information.
  • Prompt Resolution: We will dispatch an immediate replacement at no cost or issue an instant refund to your original payment method.
  • Inquiries: Contact support@kabodcrest.com or WhatsApp +234 905 380 7722 with your order reference.

4. Privacy & Data Protection

Kabod Crest Limited is committed to ethical data stewardship in accordance with the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA).

  • Information We Collect: Your full name, delivery address, email address, and phone number are gathered strictly for order fulfillment, dispatch tracking, and customer service.
  • Payment Security: All digital transactions are securely processed by Paystack (PCI-DSS Level 1 certified). Kabod Crest never receives or stores your debit card numbers, PINs, or banking credentials.
  • No Third-Party Data Sales: We never sell, rent, or lease your personal contact details to external advertisers.
