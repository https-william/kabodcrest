Frequently Addressed Questions

How do orders and delivery operate?

Orders are processed under rigorous internal hygiene controls and gentle low-temperature dehydration. Produce checkout covers your items; delivery fees are paid directly to your dispatch courier on handover.

What destinations do you deliver to?

We deliver across Nigeria and serve diaspora wholesale enquiries. Destination availability and shipping logistics for diaspora shipments are confirmed with our Trade Desk prior to dispatch.

How are payments processed?

We process secure payments exclusively through Paystack, supporting all major debit cards (Mastercard, Visa, Verve) and USSD with instant automated verification.

How can businesses initiate institutional partnerships?

Submit your collaboration brief through our contact form or reach our directors directly via trade@kabodcrest.com. Commercial confidentiality is strictly observed.
