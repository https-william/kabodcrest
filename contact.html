Frequently Addressed Questions

How does the Food Pre-Order program operate?

Pre-orders lock in initial packaging allocations for our first production harvest. No payment is forced at checkout without verified invoice instructions, and dispatch starts once packaging completes.

What destinations do you deliver to?

We deliver nationwide across all 36 Nigerian states and the FCT. For diaspora clients and international retailers, we arrange direct delivery to the UK, US, Canada, Australia, and South Africa.

How are payments processed?

For initial commercial stability, we operate verified direct bank transfer. Every order generates an itemized downloadable PDF receipt, with instant confirmation through our WhatsApp trade desk.

How can businesses initiate institutional partnerships?

Submit your collaboration brief through our contact form or reach our directors directly via kabodcrest@gmail.com. Strict commercial confidentiality is observed.
