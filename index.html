Clarity & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Direct answers regarding our Nigerian agro-produce harvests, clean-room dehydration standards, nationwide delivery, and corporate divisions.

What is Kabod Crest Limited?
Kabod Crest Limited is a registered Nigerian enterprise headquartered in Lagos. We operate across four deliberate verticals: Food Production & Agro-Processing, Events & Experiences, Hospitality & Food Services, and Strategic Business Services, built on a foundation of integrity and uncompromising quality.
What agro-produce is currently available in the Foods catalog?
Our active kitchen staples include clean-room Dehydrated Ugwu (tender fluted pumpkin leaves from Enugu, 250g at ₦2,850), pure Kaduna Dehydrated Ginger Powder (stone-ground root, 250g at ₦2,400), and heritage Jollof Rice Spice (100g at ₦2,200). Upcoming selections include Kulikuli, Stockfish, Iru locust beans, and Ogbono seeds.
How does clean-room dehydration protect farm freshness?
Our produce undergoes careful sorting and low-temperature air dehydration below 45°C. This safely reduces moisture to under 8%, preventing spoilage and bacterial growth while locking in vibrant chlorophyll, aromatic essential oils, and vital nutrients without using chemical preservatives or artificial additives.
What are your delivery locations, timelines, and shipping rates?
We ship across all 36 states of Nigeria at a flat logistics rate of ₦1,000. Orders in Lagos arrive within 24 to 48 hours, while nationwide deliveries arrive within 2 to 4 business days. We also support international air export for the African diaspora in the United Kingdom, United States, and Canada.
How does payment work on Kabod Crest?
We provide secure live checkout integrated with Paystack. You can pay instantly using all major Nigerian debit cards (Mastercard, Visa, Verve), direct bank transfer, USSD, or international cards with instant confirmation and automated tracking.
Can businesses, hotels, and restaurants purchase in bulk wholesale?
Yes. Our Business Services division partners with hospitality operators, restaurant chains, catering services, and corporate retail buyers for bulk metric allocations, private-label packaging, and contract packing. Contact our Trade Desk directly at kabodcrest@gmail.com or +234 905 380 7722.
