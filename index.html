Frequently Asked Questions

Direct answers regarding our Nigerian agro-produce harvests, clean-room dehydration standards, nationwide delivery, and corporate divisions.

What is Kabod Crest Limited?
Kabod Crest Limited is a registered Nigerian enterprise headquartered in Lagos. We operate across four deliberate verticals: Food Production & Agro-Processing, Events & Experiences, Hospitality & Food Services, and Strategic Business Services, built on a foundation of integrity and uncompromising quality.
What agro-produce is currently available in the Foods catalog?
Our active kitchen staples include hand-sorted Dehydrated Ugwu (tender fluted pumpkin leaves from Enugu, 500g at ₦10,000), pure Kaduna Dehydrated Ginger Powder (stone-ground root, 250g at ₦2,400), and heritage Jollof Rice Spice (100g at ₦2,200). Upcoming selections include Kulikuli, Stockfish, Iru locust beans, and Ogbono seeds.
How does gentle dehydration protect farm freshness?
Our produce undergoes multi-stage sorting, thorough washing, and low-temperature dehydration under 45°C. This safely reduces moisture to under 8%, preventing spoilage while locking in vibrant chlorophyll, aromatic essential oils, and natural flavor without chemical preservatives or artificial additives.
What are your delivery locations, timelines, and shipping rates?
We deliver across Nigeria and serve diaspora wholesale enquiries. Delivery logistics fees vary by destination and carrier; you pay the dispatch courier directly upon package handover. Orders in Lagos typically arrive within 24 to 48 hours, while interstate shipments arrive within 2 to 4 business days. For diaspora requests, our Sourcing Desk confirms destination availability and courier fees prior to dispatch.
How does payment work on Kabod Crest?
We provide secure live checkout integrated exclusively with Paystack. You can pay instantly using all major Nigerian and international debit cards (Mastercard, Visa, Verve) or USSD with immediate automated confirmation. Checkout charges produce subtotal only; courier delivery is paid upon delivery handover.
Can businesses, hotels, and restaurants purchase in bulk wholesale?
Yes. Kabod Crest Foods partners with hospitality operators, supermarket chains, catering services, and diaspora distributors for bulk metric allocations and contract packaging. Contact our Trade Desk directly at trade@kabodcrest.com, sales@kabodcrest.com, or +234 905 380 7722.
