Culinary Economics & Science

The 500g Secret: How 1 Pouch Becomes 3.2kg of Fresh Leaves

In diaspora supermarkets and open markets, fresh pumpkin leaves are either wilted, frozen into ice blocks, or sold with heavy stems and sand attached. Here is why Kabod Crest dehydrated Ugwu transforms your cooking:

01

The Dry Measure

500g Net Pouch = 3.5kg Fresh Harvest

Hand-sorted in Enugu, stripped of fibrous stalks, and multi-washed. Only pristine leaf blades enter our gentle drying chamber below 45°C. You get 100% usable food weight with zero stem waste.

02

3-Minute Awakening

180 Seconds in Lukewarm Water

Immerse leaves in lukewarm water. The cellular structure drinks in moisture in 3 minutes, restoring tender leaf texture, crisp bite, and vibrant emerald chlorophyll without boiling away heat-sensitive vitamins.

03

3.2kg+ Kitchen Yield

6 to 8 Family Soup Pots

One 500g pouch yields over 3.2kg of ready-to-cook fluted pumpkin leaves. That is enough to prepare 6 to 8 generous pots of Egusi, Edikang Ikong, Ogbono, or Yam pottage.

Processing Disciplines

Soil to Seal: Three Disciplines of Kabod Agro-Processing

We do not rush. Every harvest follows strict operational controls to preserve the nutritional life and vibrant taste of Nigerian soil.

Pillar 01

Hand-Sorted & Multi-Washed Clean

Harvested at morning dew from contracted growers in Enugu. Leaves are hand-stemmed and passed through sequential filtered water baths under documented quality controls. No grit, no sand, no garden dirt.

Pillar 02

Low-Temperature Dehydration (< 45°C)

Industrial heat drying scorches vegetables and turns green leaves black. Our low-temperature convection airflow slowly removes water under 45°C, preserving cellular integrity, live enzymes, and deep emerald chlorophyll.

Pillar 03

Multi-Layer Barrier Foil Sealing

Packed immediately in food-grade, light-blocking moisture barrier pouches with resealable airtight zips. Light, moisture, and oxidation stay out. Shelf-stable for over 12 months in any climate without chemical preservatives.

Clear Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Direct answers regarding our Nigerian agro-produce harvests, dehydration methods, nationwide delivery, and checkout.

What is Kabod Crest Foods?
Kabod Crest Foods is the active food production and agro-processing division of Kabod Crest Limited, based in Lagos, Nigeria. We source single-origin Nigerian staples, wash them meticulously, and dehydrate them gently under 45°C to preserve true flavor and vibrant nutrition for homes and diaspora kitchens.
What products are currently available on the active harvest shelf?
Our three active kitchen staples are Dehydrated Ugwu (fluted pumpkin leaves, 500g pouch at ₦10,000, yielding 3.2kg+ reconstituted fresh leaves), pure Kaduna Dehydrated Ginger Root Powder (250g pouch at ₦2,400), and stone-ground heritage Jollof Rice Spice (100g pouch at ₦2,200).
Why is 500g of dehydrated Ugwu priced at ₦10,000?
One 500g pouch contains the dehydrated leaf yield of over 3.5kg of fresh harvest leaves with all fibrous stalks and silt removed. When soaked in warm water for 3 minutes, it expands into over 3.2kg of ready-to-cook, tender, sand-free leaves, sufficient for 6 to 8 generous family soup pots. That works out to under ₦1,400 per massive pot of premium greens.
How does low-temperature dehydration preserve freshness?
Our produce undergoes multi-stage sorting, thorough washing, and low-temperature dehydration under 45°C. This safely reduces moisture to under 8%, preventing spoilage while locking in vibrant chlorophyll, aromatic essential oils, and natural flavor without chemical preservatives.
Where do you deliver and how are shipping fees paid?
We deliver across Lagos (24-48 hours) and nationwide across Nigeria (2-4 business days), as well as international air freight to the UK, US, and Canada. Delivery logistics fees vary by exact location and destination courier, and are paid directly to your dispatch courier upon handover.
How does payment work on Kabod Crest?
We provide secure live checkout integrated exclusively with Paystack. You can pay instantly using all major Nigerian and international cards (Mastercard, Visa, Verve) or USSD with immediate automated confirmation. Checkout charges produce subtotal only; courier delivery is paid upon delivery handover.
